      The science of dunking

      In 1998 the physicist Dr Len Fisher applied the Washburn equation to biscuits and tea, and found that tea soaks in through tiny pores between the sugar and starch. Our dunk times are kitchen-table estimates for a mug of hot builder's tea. Your mug may vary.
